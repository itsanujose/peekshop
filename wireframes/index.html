Design principles & MVP boundaries

Trust first: show stock timestamps and flag records older than 60 minutes as possibly outdated. Never invent crowd data.

Privacy and accessibility: explain location permission and provide manual area selection; support English and German, readable contrast and accessible controls.

Recovery and handoff: show alternatives when sold out; reservations expire after 60 minutes and can be cancelled. Retailer CSV updates and quick phone updates support stock freshness. Shelf feedback flags inaccurate records.

Scope: the six original shopper wireframes are shown here. Retailer portal, splash and other states are described in the PRD but not illustrated in these six figures. AI assistant, loyalty, payments and delivery are outside this MVP.

Prototype naming: the original sketches use Spice Bazaar and Bella Deli as placeholders. PRD v1.2 states that the prototype uses The Neighborhood Grocery and Feinkost & Lebensmittel instead.
