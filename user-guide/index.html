01 / Shopper guide

Getting started

Open the prototype on a phone or computer.

  1. Open PeekShop

    Wait for the green splash screen to transition to Welcome.

  2. Use the demo account

    Select Fill in demo login, then Log in. Home greets you as Raj.

  3. Explore as a new shopper

    For the intended MVP, create an account with name, email and password. Use only fictional details in the prototype.

  4. Choose your area

    For the planned MVP, allow location access or choose an area manually.

02 / Shopper guide

Find a product

Try searching for whole wheat atta.

  1. Search

    On Home, type atta, bread, cheese or tomatoes. Select a suggestion or press Enter.

  2. Browse

    Alternatively, choose a community category such as Indian, Turkish, Ukrainian or Italian.

  3. Compare stores

    Read the stock count, distance, hours and last-updated timestamp.

  4. Filter results

    Try In stock, Open now, Under 1 km and Quiet to narrow the list.

03 / Shopper guide

Choose the right store

Use the store details to plan your trip.

  1. Open a store

    Check opening hours, stock count and crowd level.

  2. Find the shelf

    Look at the aisle and shelf information and floor plan where available.

  3. Save a favourite

    Tap the heart to save the store; use Saved to revisit it.

04 / Shopper guide

When an item is sold out

Avoid an unnecessary trip.

  1. Find a substitute

    On a sold-out result, tap the green Similar here line if available.

  2. Compare the alternative

    Check its stock, pack size and store before travelling.

  3. Try another store

    If no suitable alternative appears, look for another store with the original product.

  4. Save for later

    If nothing suitable is available, save the product and check again later.

05 / Shopper guide

Reserve and collect

Optional one-hour hold.

  1. Reserve

    Open an eligible in-stock item and select Reserve for 1 hour.

  2. Check confirmation

    Note the pickup code and hold deadline. The prototype code is illustrative.

  3. Collect

    In the intended service, show the pickup code to store staff before expiry; someone else can collect using the code.

  4. Cancel if needed

    In the prototype, tap the reservation control again to cancel. In the intended service, cancellation or expiry releases the stock; a reminder is planned 15 minutes before expiry.

06 / Shopper guide

Navigate to the store

Follow the illustrated walking route.

  1. Start

    Tap Get directions, then Start walking directions.

  2. Follow the route

    Use Next step to advance the simulated directions.

  3. Arrive

    Check the pickup code or product shelf location shown on arrival.

  4. Give feedback

    In the planned MVP, answer Was it on the shelf? A No response flags the record and notifies the retailer.

07 / Shopper guide

Saved items and sign-out

Manage your session.

  1. Open Saved

    Review saved stores and products.

  2. Sign out

    Tap the door-and-arrow logout icon to return to Welcome.

  3. Reset

    Saved items and reservations are cleared on logout; prototype accounts are temporary and reset on reload.

08 / Planned retailer workflow

Retailer portal

Planned MVP workflow — not a verified interactive prototype feature.

  1. Create store profile

    Provide address, opening hours and photos, and await approval.

  2. Upload inventory

    Prepare a CSV with product, quantity, aisle and shelf. Review rejected rows and correct them.

  3. Update stock

    Use the planned phone-friendly portal to change a quantity or mark an item sold out.

  4. Handle reservations

    Set aside reserved items, verify pickup codes and mark collected.

  5. Maintain freshness

    Respond to stale-stock reminders and inaccurate-stock feedback. POS integration is limited to a proposed pilot.

Frequently asked questions

What do stock labels mean?

In stock means available; low stock means three or fewer units remain; sold out means none remain. In the intended service, records older than 60 minutes are labelled May be outdated. Always check the timestamp.

What if crowd or shelf information is missing?

Recent crowd data and aisle or shelf information are shown only when available. Do not assume a store is quiet if no crowd signal is shown.

Can I pay here or request delivery?

No. Payments, delivery, personalised AI shopping assistance and loyalty integration are outside this MVP scope.

What if I cannot find a product?

Try another spelling, a community category, or remove restrictive filters. Check a similar item or save the product for later.

Where is PeekShop intended to operate?

The planned pilot covers Neukölln, Kreuzberg, Wedding and Schöneberg in Berlin, with English and German support.
